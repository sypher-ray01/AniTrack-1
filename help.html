Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about using AniTrack, including frequently asked questions and keyboard shortcuts.

Frequently Asked Questions

You can add anime to your Watchlist from any anime card or detail page by clicking the "+ Add" button. You'll be prompted to select a status (Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, or Plan to Watch).
While on the Watchlist page, look for the "Share" button in the toolbar. This will generate a unique text token that you can send to your friends. They can use the "Import" function to view your list!
The Admin Panel is a restricted area of AniTrack used for content moderation, such as reviewing user reports and making global announcements. Only accounts with the "Admin" privilege flag can enter this view.
On any currently airing anime's detail page, click the "Remind Me" bell icon next to the next episode air date. You will need to allow browser notification permissions when prompted.
Navigate to the Clubs directory page and click the primary "Create Club" button in the top right. You can set a custom banner, description, tags, and choose whether the club is Public or Private.
AniTrack uses the Web Notifications API and Service Workers. This means as long as you have the browser open (or the PWA installed on your device), you will receive a native push notification precisely when the episode drops.
Yes! You can export your entire Watchlist as a CSV file from the Watchlist page. You can also export your raw JSON profile data from the Account Settings section in your Profile.
On Chrome or Edge, click the "Install" icon in your URL bar, or open the browser menu and select "Install AniTrack". On iOS/Safari, tap the Share button and select "Add to Home Screen".
