# Privacy Policy

**AniTrack**
Last updated: March 2026

---

## 1. Overview

AniTrack is a client-side web application. All data you enter (watchlist, reviews, club activity, account details) is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. No data is transmitted to or stored on any external server operated by AniTrack.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### Data You Provide
- Username and password (password is hashed using SHA-256 before storage — never stored in plain text)
- Watchlist entries and episode progress
- Reviews, ratings, and comments
- Club posts and discussion threads
- Profile information (bio, avatar)
- Reminder preferences

### Data Automatically Used
- Anime metadata is fetched from the Jikan API (api.jikan.moe), a third-party service. Please review [Jikan's policies](https://jikan.moe/) for information about their data handling.

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect any personal data on any server
- We do not use cookies
- We do not use third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics)
- We do not run advertising networks
- We do not track your behaviour across sessions or devices

---

## 3. Data Storage

All AniTrack data is stored in your browser's localStorage under keys prefixed with `at_`. This data:
- Exists only on your device
- Is deleted when you clear your browser storage
- Is not accessible to AniTrack or any third party
- Is limited to approximately 5MB by your browser

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

AniTrack loads resources from the following third-party CDNs:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Jikan API (api.jikan.moe) | Anime metadata | https://jikan.moe |
| Google Fonts | Inter typeface | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| jsDelivr CDN | Chart.js library | https://www.jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net |
| unpkg CDN | Lucide icons | https://unpkg.com |

These services may log your IP address per their own policies.

---

## 5. Web Notifications

If you enable episode reminders, AniTrack will request permission to send browser notifications. This is handled entirely by your browser. No notification data is sent to any server. You can revoke this permission at any time in your browser settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

AniTrack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

---

## 7. India — Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023

As a client-side application that does not collect or process personal data on any server, AniTrack's data processing obligations under the DPDP Act 2023 are minimal. All user data remains on the user's own device and is not processed by any data fiduciary.
